Only glyph outlines needed for the motto are embedded. Pen-mask centerlines are generated offline by scripts/build-motto-lettering.py. The original font is not loaded by visitors.

## Chinese subtitle

`chinese-art.json` contains only the filled calligraphic outlines used by the Chinese subtitle. Source: https://github.com/skishore/makemeahanzi/blob/bddc96d41bef78427ed0e034e9f7e31d71fd1b92/graphics.txt . Derived from Arphic PL KaitiM GB / PL UKai, under the Arphic Public License. Full notices are retained in `docs/licenses/makemeahanzi/`. The Chinese subtitle fades in as a whole rather than animating individual strokes.
